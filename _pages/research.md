I use molecular dynamics (MD) simulations to study protein conformational dynamics,
with a focus on disordered and flexible proteins. My broader interests include
machine learning force fields, ML-based trajectory analysis, and biomolecular
condensate formation.

---

## Current Projects

**Large Conformational Dynamics of CaMKII**  
CaMKII (Ca²⁺/calmodulin-dependent protein kinase II) undergoes dramatic structural
transitions between compact and extended states that are critical to its regulatory
function. I use coarse-grained and all-atom MD simulations to characterize the
mechanism and free energy landscape of this transition.

**Disordered Protein Interactions** *(side project)*  
Intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs) form highly dynamic, transient complexes
that are difficult to resolve experimentally. I am investigating how disordered
regions mediate protein–protein interactions using MD simulations.

## Completed Projects

**Coupled Binding and Folding of Flavivirus Protease NS2B/NS3**  
The NS2B/NS3 protease is an essential drug target for flaviviruses including dengue
and Zika. Using topology-based modeling, I studied its folding pathways and the
role of the NS2B linker in modulating protease activity.  
→ *Published in J. Phys. Chem. B, 2026*
