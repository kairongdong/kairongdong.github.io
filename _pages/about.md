I am a Ph.D. student in Computational Biophysics and Molecular Modeling at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, working with Prof. Jianhan Chen. My research focuses on intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs), protein folding, and large-scale conformational dynamics using molecular dynamic simulations and machine learning methods.

I received my M.A. in Biophysics from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Institute of Biophysics, where I studied the structure and function of Vaccinia Virus helicase-primase D5. Prior to that, I earned my B.S. in Biotechnology from Central China Normal University.
